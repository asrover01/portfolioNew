About Me

Hello, I'm Mark

I’ve completed a responsive design course that taught me how to build mobile-friendly and desktop websites using HTML and CSS. I’m currently studying JavaScript, as I enjoy building, whether I’m coding a responsive website, piecing together Lego or building structures in games, I am a builder at heart. I love taking a blank canvas or a pile of pieces and turning them into something functional and visually engaging. Throughout my self study, I've gained a greater understanding of how websites work behind the scenes. I enjoy problem-solving and developing new skills that help me grow.


Outside of coding, I value a healthy balance of gaming, reading, and walking to stay refreshed and focused. I’m drawn to games that challenge me with strategy, exploration, and puzzles, while reading allows me to unwind while continuing to learn new things. Walking serves as a great way to disconnect, clear my head, and stay active. I’ve also recently taken up adult colouring books, which require a high level of focus and intricate attention to detail. Together, these activities provide the perfect balance of mental stimulation and relaxation, keeping me motivated on my web development journey. To round things out, I’ve also joined local wellbeing cafes to connect with others and enjoy more social group experiences.
